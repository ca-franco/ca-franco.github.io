About Me

Hello! My name is Franco. I'm from India and currently living and working in Bengaluru. I am a Software Engineer/Architect, a father of two sons, a husband, and a son. I completed my Engineering in Electronics and Communications in 2004 and earned a postgraduate diploma in embedded systems from CDAC Trivandrum, India.

Early in my career, I transitioned to a Computer Science role. I have been with my current company for nearly 14 years. I decided to document my interests and experiences here.

Programming is one of my passions, but I also enjoy listening to music, especially on YouTube Music, and I have a large collection of CDs. I spend a lot of my time learning to play the piano.

I love cooking authentic food for my family and friends. Traveling is another passion of mine, and you can read about my trips on my blog.

Feel free to contact me. I'm always open to new connections.
